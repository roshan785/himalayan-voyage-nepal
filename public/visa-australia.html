About this visa

Australia visitor visas (subclass 600) and student visas (subclass 500) are lodged online. We help build the Genuine Temporary Entrant evidence that most refusals hinge on.

Checklist

Documents you will need

  • Passport valid for the trip
  • Subclass 600/500 application
  • Photograph
  • Bank statements and proof of funds
  • Employment / study evidence
  • Health insurance (required for students)
  • Statement of purpose (students)
  • Flight and accommodation details
Process

How it works

  • 1. Choose the right subclass
  • 2. Create an ImmiAccount
  • 3. Upload documents
  • 4. Complete health and character checks if required
  • 5. Submit and pay
  • 6. Track the decision
