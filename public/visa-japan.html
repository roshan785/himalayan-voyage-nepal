About this visa

Japan tourist visas require a clear itinerary and, in most cases, a certificate of eligibility is not needed for short visits. We prepare the full itinerary and financial evidence.

Checklist

Documents you will need

  • Passport valid for the trip
  • Visa application form
  • Photograph 45x45mm
  • Daily itinerary
  • Bank statements / tax documents
  • Employment certificate
  • Flight and hotel bookings
  • Guarantor documents if applicable
Process

How it works

  • 1. Prepare the itinerary
  • 2. Complete the application
  • 3. Submit at the embassy or authorised agency
  • 4. Pay the fee
  • 5. Wait for processing
  • 6. Collect the passport
