About this visa

A Schengen visa lets you travel across 27 European countries on a single visa. The application must be made at the consulate of the country where you will spend the most nights.

Checklist

Documents you will need

  • Passport valid 3+ months beyond return
  • Schengen application form
  • Two recent photographs
  • Travel insurance covering €30,000
  • Flight and hotel bookings
  • Bank statements for 6 months
  • Employment proof and leave letter
  • Day-by-day itinerary
Process

How it works

  • 1. Identify the main destination country
  • 2. Book a consulate appointment
  • 3. Prepare the full document set
  • 4. Submit biometrics and documents
  • 5. Pay the visa fee
  • 6. Collect the visa or passport
