About this visa

The United States B1/B2 visa covers tourism, family visits and business meetings. We help you assemble a convincing file, prepare for the interview and understand the fee structure before you pay anything.

Checklist

Documents you will need

  • Passport valid 6+ months with 2 blank pages
  • DS-160 confirmation page
  • Two 5x5cm photographs (white background)
  • Bank statements for the last 6 months
  • Proof of employment or business
  • Property / asset documents (if any)
  • Confirmed return ticket and hotel bookings
  • Travel itinerary and cover letter
Process

How it works

  • 1. Eligibility assessment
  • 2. Complete the DS-160 form
  • 3. Pay the visa fee
  • 4. Book the interview slot
  • 5. Attend the interview with documents
  • 6. Collect the passport with the decision
