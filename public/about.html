Our Story

Creating memorable journeys since 2011

We started as a small trekking desk in Pokhara and grew into a full-service travel agency handling tours, trekking, adventure activities and customized international holidays. Our team has walked the trails, stayed in the teahouses and worked with local operators \u2014 so the advice you get is first-hand.

Whether you are planning a family holiday, a Himalayan trek or a visa application for study or work abroad, we handle each file with the same care: clear pricing, realistic itineraries and honest guidance.
