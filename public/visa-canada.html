About this visa

Canada offers visitor visas, study permits and work permits. We review your purpose of travel and financial documents carefully, since these are the most common reasons an application is refused.

Checklist

Documents you will need

  • Passport valid for the whole trip
  • IMM 5257 application form
  • Two photographs as per specification
  • Proof of funds (bank statements)
  • Employment / study proof
  • Invitation letter (if visiting family)
  • Travel itinerary
  • Family information form
Process

How it works

  • 1. Determine the right permit category
  • 2. Create an IRCC online account
  • 3. Upload documents and pay biometrics
  • 4. Give biometrics at the VAC
  • 5. Submit the application
  • 6. Track the decision online
