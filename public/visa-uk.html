About this visa

The UK Standard Visitor visa covers tourism, business and family visits up to 6 months. We help with the online application, financial evidence and the appointment at the visa application centre.

Checklist

Documents you will need

  • Passport with a blank page
  • Completed online application
  • Bank statements for 6 months
  • Employment / business proof
  • Accommodation details
  • Return flight booking
  • Invitation letter (if applicable)
  • Photograph as per UKVI rules
Process

How it works

  • 1. Complete the online application
  • 2. Pay the visa fee and health surcharge if applicable
  • 3. Book a biometric appointment
  • 4. Attend the appointment
  • 5. Wait for the decision
  • 6. Collect the passport
