About this visa

China tourist (L) and business (M) visas need an invitation or a confirmed itinerary. We guide you through the application form and the biometric appointment.

Checklist

Documents you will need

  • Passport valid 6+ months
  • Visa application form (COVA)
  • Photograph 33x48mm
  • Invitation letter from China
  • Flight and hotel bookings
  • Bank statements
  • Employment proof
  • Previous visas (if any)
Process

How it works

  • 1. Complete the COVA form
  • 2. Book an appointment at the visa centre
  • 3. Submit documents and give biometrics
  • 4. Pay the fee
  • 5. Wait for processing
  • 6. Collect the visa
