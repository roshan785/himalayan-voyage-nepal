Message from the CEO

Why we do this, and how we treat every traveller who trusts us with their journey.

Meet the Team

"Travel has helped us understand the meaning of life, and it has helped us become better people. Each time we travel, we see the world with new eyes."

When we opened our first desk in Pokhara, we had one rule: never sell a trip we would not take ourselves. That rule still decides everything \u2014 which hotels we recommend, which routes we suggest in which season, and when we tell a traveller that a plan simply will not work within their budget.

Nepal is not only Everest. It is the quiet of Rara Lake, the wide valley of Dhorpatan, the fog over the Annapurna foothills at dawn. Our job is to help you find the version of Nepal \u2014 or of the world \u2014 that is right for you, and then to remove every avoidable problem between you and that experience.

We are deliberately small. You will speak to the same person from your first enquiry to your return home, and that person answers the phone during your trip. That is the whole promise.

RC
Roshan ChauChief Executive Officer
